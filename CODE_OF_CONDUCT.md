# Code of Conduct

We want CDPA-LLMs to be a respectful, evidence-oriented community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, welcome good-faith questions, protect private research data, and avoid overstating what an assessment can establish.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or participant data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. This policy applies in repository discussions and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
